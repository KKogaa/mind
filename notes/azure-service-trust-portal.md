The Azure Service Trust Portal is Microsoft's public portal that provides transparency about Azure's security, privacy, and compliance practices.

## What is the Service Trust Portal?
The Service Trust Portal is a one-stop resource where you can access detailed information about MIcrosoft security, privacy and compliance measures across Azure and other Microsoft cloud services. It's designed to help organizations make informed decisions about using Microsoft cloud services.

## Key Information Available
### Compliance Reports
The portal provides access to independent audit reports, compliance certifications, and assessments that demonstrate how Microsoft cloud services meet various regulatory standards like SOC, ISO, PCI DSS, and regional compliance frameworks.

### Trust Documents
You can find whitepapers, FAQs, and detailed documentation about Microsoft approach to security, privacy and compliance. This includes information about data protection, security controls, and risk management practices.

### Industry and Regional Compliance
The portal organizes compliance information by industry and by region to help you understand how Azure meets specific regulatory requirements in your sector or location.
