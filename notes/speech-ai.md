# Speech AI

Speech recognition and speech synthesis. One of the workloads in [[ai-workloads]]; delivered on Azure by the Speech tool listed in [[microsoft-foundry]].

## Speech recognition (speech-to-text)

Converts spoken language into written text.

- Voice assistants.
- Can identify different languages and accents — an [[responsible-ai]] inclusiveness concern.
- Some systems support real-time transcription for live conversations.
- Speaker recognition.

## Speech synthesis (text-to-speech)

Converts written text into spoken audio.

- Respond using a natural-sounding voice.
- Navigation systems.
- Some systems support **neural voices**, which use deep learning to produce more realistic speech.
- Can train the AI on your own voice.
